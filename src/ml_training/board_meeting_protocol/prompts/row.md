Compare one past meeting. Report the transformation, not a rewrite. Write the comparison once, then stop.

Writing: raw notes become the finished protocol. Say what was kept, rewritten, dropped, or added, and why. Name sections that appear in the finished protocol even though the notes barely mention them. Those may be carried from meeting to meeting.

Reviewing: the draft becomes the finished protocol. Say what changed and why. If a feedback email is included, say why the reviewer wrote about some points and stayed silent on others. Judge how pedantic that is: small wording, or only decisions and gaps.

Give a conclusion only when the documents support it. If a part is unclear, say so and put this on its own line:

<HIGH UNCERTAINTY; FILL IN MANUALLY OR PROVIDE OPTIONAL CONTEXT>

Do not invent a rule. Do not draft the new document.
